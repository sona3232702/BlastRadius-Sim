🐣 How BlastRadius Sim Works

1. Dependency Graph: Pods talk to each other across columns (Edge → Gateway → Services → DB).

2. Cascading Failure: When a pod crashes, its dependent caller services lose connection, expanding the blast radius.

3. One-Click Mitigation: Apply circuit breakers or retry fallbacks to isolate broken nodes and keep critical SLA paths online!
